Where should you wash your car right now?

WashRadar is a free web app for finding car washes in Toronto and the Greater Toronto Area. Compare drive distance, estimated queues, wash time and price when available. Unknown information stays marked unknown.

Search by city, postal code or address. Choose touchless, automatic, tunnel, soft-cloth, self-serve or hand-wash options when verified data is available.

Community reports help estimate waits; they do not guarantee a live queue. Check business details before travelling.
